# Kept Sans

`KeptSans.woff2` and `KeptSans-Italic.woff2` are Latin-script subsets of
[Google Sans v14.000](https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans/releases/tag/v14.000).
The source is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see [OFL.txt](OFL.txt).
The upstream trademark notice is preserved in [TRADEMARKS.txt](TRADEMARKS.txt).

The WOFF2 files were generated from the release's Roman and Italic variable
TTF files with fontTools. They retain the weight axis from 400 to 700 and the
optical-size and grade axes. The subset targets glyphs in U+0020-U+024F,
U+0300-U+036F, U+1E00-U+1EFF, U+2000-U+206F, U+20A0-U+20CF, and
U+2100-U+214F. This includes the glyphs needed for the Latin-script languages
listed in the PR. The derived font's internal family and vendor names were
changed to Kept Sans and KEPT to avoid presenting the modified files as
Google's original font software.
